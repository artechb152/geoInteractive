# Dead reckoning — final visual polish, 2026-11-01

The `before` and `after` images are **actual static-production browser captures**,
at 1440 × 1122, driven through the normal controls. They do not use development
camera placement or state hooks. Both 400-pair captures use the same location
and initial look direction. Airborne dust evolves with time, so its exact
pattern is not frozen between runs.

stones and boulder. Radius/height bounds, scatter, burial and contact shadows
stay compatible. The crossing's rise is spread into a gentler ramp; eroded
notches break up the bank lip. Ground material gets a modest local contrast
increase. Camera/FOV, visibility/dust attenuation, 1.4-second movement and UI
are unchanged.
