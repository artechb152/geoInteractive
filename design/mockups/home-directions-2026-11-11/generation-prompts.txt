01-cinematic-landscape — נוף קולנועי

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE exceptionally art-directed, high-fidelity desktop homepage website mockup image for a Hebrew RTL interactive military geography learning platform, brand "גיאוגרפיה צבאית" with small GEO9900 identifier. This is an entirely new design from a blank canvas, NOT a reskin of any earlier image. Landscape desktop composition about 1440 x 1122, sharp legible real UI, flat front-on screenshot filling the image edge to edge, no browser chrome, no device, no outer presentation board. The imagery must feel technically ambitious and immersive, but preserve an immediately usable learning entrance.
Content invariants: exactly ONE carousel containing exactly THREE current course chapter cards, ordered RIGHT TO LEFT: "01" "תבליט, תכסית וטופוגרפיה"; "02" "קרטוגרפיה וקריאת מפות"; "03" "ניווטים". Distinct visual per chapter: terrain relief, topographic map, route/compass. Make this a real prominent carousel with subtle side arrows and three-position pagination, not three unrelated feature cards. Include "פרקי הקורס" as carousel heading and an obvious "המשך ללמוד" main CTA. No extra chapter carousels, no coming soon rows, no fictional course counts or progress percentages. All important Hebrew text must be correctly written, right aligned and unmirrored; typeset in modern highly readable bold Hebrew sans. Maps and numerals remain geographically correct and unmirrored. Keep interface copy minimal. ABSOLUTELY NO benefits/features grid, NO four icon cards, NO row about interactive maps/field skills/operational thinking/information layers. No stock dashboard statistics, no pricing, no marketing icon grid, no cartoon toy diorama with orange flag. Typography and artwork must feel like a finished award-level designed real site, not concept art with fake UI sprinkled over it. 
ART DIRECTION 1 — IMMERSIVE CINEMATIC LANDSCAPE. A breathtaking full-bleed, photorealistic aerial 3D mountain valley in the Israeli desert at blue-hour dawn, enormous sculpted ridges, dry river winding into the horizon, warm thin sunlight catching edges, deep charcoal-olive shadows, atmospheric volumetric mist. No separate square floating terrain object: the WORLD is the entire page background. Only a few exquisite thin topographic overlays and one warm amber route cross the terrain, implying a real interactive flyover. Composition unlike a normal two-column landing: centered large white Hebrew headline in the upper-middle negative sky, text "לראות את השטח." and below "להבין את המרחב." Elegant quiet top navigation, brand upper right, understated course link upper left. Main small high-quality amber rectangular CTA centered below headline. Huge open landscape, almost no UI in the middle 50 percent, convey scale and cinematic depth. The single three-chapter carousel is a wide opaque near-black horizontal filmstrip anchored across the BOTTOM quarter, with three landscape cinematic chapter tiles, substantial image portions, fine cream labels, large tasteful chapter numbers, active rightmost chapter thin amber underline, small arrows at the outer sides. Header "פרקי הקורס" just above filmstrip at the right. Subtle thin elevation scale at far left as one quiet environmental detail. Color treatment restrained charcoal/olive/ivory/warm amber, no neon or purple. Premium film-title typography, realistic WebGL landscape, dramatic calm, spectacular but accessible.
