# פרומפטים לנכסי המקור

כל הנכסים נוצרו ב־image_gen המובנה. להלן פרומפטים לשחזור הכיוון; הם אינם מבטיחים תוצאה זהה. לרקע ולמטוס שימש המוקאפ 01-operations-field-realistic.png כתמונת ייחוס. יתר הנכסים נוצרו בנפרד עם תאורה וכיוון תואמים.

## רקע

Create a standalone clean 4:3 photographic background plate based on the left landscape in the reference mockup. Human eye-level viewpoint from a rocky Mediterranean coastal hillside. Sea on the left, rugged olive-green hills to the right, pale blue sky in the upper third, beige foreground rocks and scrub. Natural late-afternoon sunlight from upper left. Preserve a clear dirt track in the middle-right region for a vehicle and an open area for a communications mast. Remove all UI, text, orange paths, rings, military objects, aircraft, ships, vehicles, satellite and communications mast. No aerial or isometric perspective, no miniature, no pedestal. Full-bleed natural-color photographic scene.

## מטוס

One isolated realistic modern gray jet aircraft in flight, landing gear retracted, nose pointing right. Slight underside visible as photographed from the ground, mostly side profile. Entire wings and tail visible. Genuine transparent PNG alpha outside the object, no background or scenery, no contrail, no text, no insignia, no UI, no ring. Warm natural afternoon sunlight from upper left, neutral realistic metal. Wide framing with transparent margin, sharp compositing silhouette.

## ספינה

Create one photorealistic compositing asset: a generic gray small naval patrol ship, broadside three-quarter view, bow pointing right. Real photographic natural proportions, warm afternoon sunlight upper left, neutral gray metal, subtle top deck visible. Whole ship including antenna and mast visible, 12% margin. True TRANSPARENT PNG alpha everywhere outside object, not a baked checkerboard. No text, hull numbers or flags. No background, water, wake, rings, overlays or UI. Only above-water hull down to horizontal waterline, not a dry dock or underwater hull. Wide horizontal composition. Suitable for compositing as a small offshore ship in a natural Mediterranean coastal scene shot from a nearby hillside. No miniature or isometric aesthetic.

## כלי קרקעי

One standalone photorealistic transparent PNG compositing asset of a generic olive-drab wheeled armored personnel carrier, 8 wheels, closed hatches, no people, no insignia. Facing RIGHT in side three-quarter photographic view, seen from approximately vehicle-roof height showing only modest top surfaces, suitable compositing onto a hillside dirt road. Entire vehicle including all tires with 12% padding. Warm natural afternoon light from upper left, realistic dusty olive paint, matte metal and rubber. True transparent alpha outside object. No terrain, no ground patch, no smoke or dust, no ring, no text, no orange effects. Thin soft contact shadow only immediately under tires allowed, no rectangular background. No isometric or toy look. Wide landscape framing.

## לוויין

Generate one isolated realistic communications satellite for an educational symbolic HUD inset, genuine TRANSPARENT PNG alpha background. Single satellite entire object visible centered with 15% padding. Compact silver and warm gold body, two symmetrical dark blue-gray solar panel wings extending diagonally horizontally, realistic detailed materials, natural warm sunlight from upper left. Three-quarter view. No space backdrop, no earth, no stars, no smoke, no glow, no ring, no lettering, no frame, no ground. Completely transparent all around and in all gaps. Photorealistic cutout asset, not cartoon. Used as a small schematic inset above a coastal photo, not as an actually visible huge satellite in the sky.

## תורן

One isolated realistic portable field communications antenna mast for compositing onto a Mediterranean hillside photograph. True transparent PNG alpha. Slim metal lattice tower, two modest directional radio dishes, compact olive equipment cabinet at base. Fully visible whole object from base to antenna tip, front three-quarter view at human eye level, natural warm afternoon light from upper left. Clear crisp cutout edges and transparent empty gaps inside lattice. IMPORTANT: no background haze, no bloom, no glow, no studio backdrop, no semitransparent gray cloud around object, no ground, no sky, no shadows beyond base, no text, no orange UI. Tall portrait canvas with 15% padding. Natural real materials and geometry. It will represent a communication node in an educational cyber overlay, not a giant server in landscape.
