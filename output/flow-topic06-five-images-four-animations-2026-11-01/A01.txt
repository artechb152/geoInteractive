משך בימוי מוצע: 8 שניות.

Animate from the supplied unmarked terrain start frame to the supplied planning-map end frame in one clear educational sequence, approximately eight seconds. The viewer must recognize one unchanged fictional location represented first as terrain and then as a map. Use one full-frame view at any moment; no split screen or map inset.

Begin with a brief clean hold on the realistic landscape. Smoothly lift the camera toward a true overhead view. Resolve the existing hill into nested contours, the same three palms into three map symbols, the same Y junction into its three road arms, and the same building into its rectangular footprint. The blue navigator becomes one blue departure symbol at the corresponding southwest location. This is a change in representation, not terrain physically flattening or landmarks moving. Finish the transition early enough to spend most of the clip on planning.

On the full-frame map, draw the broad gray dashed shortcut first, visibly crossing the steep hill contours. Then draw the thick orange lower-ground alternative with a strong traveling front, sweeping around the hill's south side, passing the palms and turning north to the same junction. The line growth should feel as tangible and noticeable as the orange boundary growing across the landscape in lesson 2. Neither route constructs a physical road.

Settle the choice on the orange route. Add the two hollow checkpoint discs one after the other, large and deliberate. The blue departure symbol remains stationary. Hold the complete supplied end frame for roughly the final second. Preserve all geography and paper styling. No walking, growing hill, new trees, compass, numeric comparison, safety guarantee, GPS effects, text, captions, speech, music, logo or watermark. The teaching action must work silently.
